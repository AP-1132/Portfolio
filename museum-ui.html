Museum Exhibit UI Mockup

Technology and Skills Used

UI/UX, Wireframe prototyping, usability testing, Adobe XD

Project Overview

Link to Prototype

I designed and prototyped the user interface for a fictional interactive museum exhibit about the Marvel Cinematic Universe. I applied Gestalt principles to help visitors navigate the exhibit and understand how its content fits together. I used common region to group related information within shared visual boundaries, continuity to guide visitors' eyes through the content in a natural order, and symmetry to create balanced layouts across exhibit screens. I also conducted usability testing on the prototype and incorporated my findings into the final design.
